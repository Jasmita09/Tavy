Source: original music composed for TΛVY and synthesised from scratch by scripts/audio/render-soundtrack.mts
(additive piano and bells, detuned-saw pads, sine bass, synthesised drums, filtered-noise rain, crowd and
risers, a Freeverb-style reverb). No samples, loops, recordings, presets or third-party melodies are used;
the motif and chord progressions were written for this project. The note taps on the landing page are
synthesised live in the browser from the same bell recipe.

Reproduce: node scripts/audio/render-soundtrack.mts  (deterministic; encodes with macOS afconvert)
Timing: components/audio/score.ts (shared by the renderer and the site, so visuals and music stay in step)
